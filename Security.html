04 — Security, Policy & Continuity

Security from Day One.
Not bolted on later.

🔥 Perimeter & endpoints

Fortinet FortiGate 200F NGFW — IPS, app control, web filtering, SD-WAN. CrowdStrike Falcon / Defender EDR on all workstations, USB control, 802.1X NAC.

👁️ Detection & encryption

Wazuh / FortiAnalyzer SIEM. TLS 1.3 in transit, AES-256 + BitLocker at rest. Monthly Nessus scans, automated WSUS patching.

🔐 Access & identity

Active Directory + Azure AD Connect, role-based access, MFA for remote/admin, unique accounts, quarterly reviews, same-day leaver disablement.

Policies & SOPs — so 3 ICT staff can run it consistently

Acceptable Use Access Control & Passwords Data Protection & Records Backup & Storage Network Security Asset Management Change Management Supplier Access

Six SOPs: logging incidents • creating / removing accounts • daily backup checks & restores • finding a missing file • replacing failed equipment • giving a supplier access.

Business continuity — recovery targets

Service RTO RPO Approach
Municipal / internet link 1 hour — Switch to backup connection
Core network equipment 2 hours — Swap spare unit (SOP 5)
Patient records storage 2 hours 24 hours Restore from daily backup (SOP 3)
Supply chain / supplier access 8 hours 24 hours Manual orders, then restore
Finance & billing 24 hours 24 hours Restore + capture missed entries

Manual downtime procedures in every ward: paper forms, phone/runner for urgent results, printed contact lists. Continuity exercises at least twice a year; restore tests every quarter.
