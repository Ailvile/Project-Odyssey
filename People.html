07 — People & Risks

Built for the people
who will run it.

ICT team (3)

ICT Support Lead — coordination, approvals, continuity coordinator.
Network & Systems Technician — network, security monitoring, backups.
End-User Support Technician — service desk, accounts, assets.

Stakeholders

Municipality (sponsor) • Hospital (end-user) • Openserve (carrier) • 600 staff • ~100 patients/day • Suppliers via Intralink • Odyssey team.

Top risks, mitigated

Openserve monopoly → dual paths + strong SLA.
Power instability → UPS + generator + solar.
1:200 ICT ratio → SLA, remote monitoring, cross-training.
Ransomware / POPIA → security-first design, backups, audits.

The payoff: One kilometre of fibre. A hospital that never loses a file again. Safer care, faster admin, and a model for rural healthcare digital transformation in South Africa.

Sources: Project Odyssey Overview, Project Charter (31 Aug 2026) & Task 2 Project Solution — Sekhukhune District Municipality / New Jane Furse Memorial Hospital.
