01 — The story

The hospital the district relies on
was running on paper.

🗂️ Missing files

Paper-based records meant files went missing — a “great concern” for patient safety and continuity of care. Care was delayed while staff searched for folders.

💻 15 computers for 600 people

One ageing computer for every 40 staff, and only 3 printers for the whole hospital. Documentation became a bottleneck.

🛡️ No security

No firewall, no endpoint protection, no SIEM, no controlled access. Patient data and hospital operations were exposed.

The opportunity: the municipal offices are only 1 km away — with a basic network that can be extended. Odyssey bridges that last kilometre, and the digital divide with it.

02 — The solution

One converged network.
Logically separated. Built to last.

A

Connectivity

1 km single-mode OS2 fibre, 24-core armoured, dual lead-in paths — municipal trench + aerial redundancy. 1 Gbps Openserve Metro Fibre, 200 Mbps LTE failover, scalable to 10 Gbps. SLA >99.5%, 4-hour MTTR.

B

Digital care

Bahmni / OpenMRS EHR — registration, OPD, IPD, lab, pharmacy, radiology, billing — plus Patient Administration, finance, supply chain, HR/payroll and a Mirth Connect / HL7 FHIR integration engine for nurse call, CCTV and security.

C

Sustainability

Train-the-trainer for all 600 staff, SOPs, a Business Continuity Plan, 5-year lifecycle and TCO planning, solar PV + inverter backup, and municipal shared services.

What gets delivered

  • Connectivity infrastructure — 1 km fibre, scalable to 10 Gbps
  • Hardware — 50+ workstations, 12 MFPs, servers, switches, Wi-Fi 6 APs, UPS
  • Software — EHR, PAS, finance, supply chain, HR, messaging engine
  • Security — NGFW, EDR, SIEM, encryption, biometrics
  • Training & knowledge transfer — ICT staff + all 600 users
  • Business continuity — automated backups, offsite storage, DR plan

04 — Security, policy & continuity

Security from Day One.
Not bolted on later.

🔥 Perimeter & endpoints

Fortinet FortiGate 200F NGFW — IPS, app control, web filtering, SD-WAN. CrowdStrike Falcon / Defender EDR on all workstations, USB control, 802.1X NAC.

👁️ Detection & encryption

Wazuh / FortiAnalyzer SIEM. TLS 1.3 in transit, AES-256 + BitLocker at rest. Monthly Nessus scans, automated WSUS patching.

🔐 Access & identity

Active Directory + Azure AD Connect, role-based access, MFA for remote/admin, unique accounts, quarterly reviews, same-day leaver disablement.

Policies & SOPs — so 3 ICT staff can run it consistently

Acceptable UseAccess Control & PasswordsData Protection & RecordsBackup & StorageNetwork SecurityAsset ManagementChange ManagementSupplier Access

Six SOPs cover the daily reality: logging incidents • creating / removing accounts • daily backup checks & restores • finding a missing file • replacing failed equipment • giving a supplier access.

Top risks, mitigated

Openserve monopoly → dual paths + strong SLA.
Power instability → UPS + generator + solar.
1:200 ICT ratio → SLA, remote monitoring, cross-training.
Ransomware / POPIA → security-first design, backups, audits.
